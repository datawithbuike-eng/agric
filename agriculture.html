Investing In Agriculture

Global population growth, a changing climate and rising household incomes in emerging markets are putting pressure on the world’s most critical resource – food. For more than 10 years we’ve combined farming expertise, investment management discipline and a deep understanding of global commodities markets ‘in-house’, to become one of United Kingdom’s largest diversified farmers and a major investor in Brazilian agriculture. Looking across countries, climatic regions, commodity types and target markets, our team of agriculture specialists selectively acquires and actively manages prime farmland to create sustainable large scale, diversified farming enterprises to feed a growing population.

Today, Legacy Strategic and its managed funds responsibly invest in and operate 4.7 million hectares of farmland – producing red meat, grains and oil seeds. Inflation Hedging

Finding good cover from inflation can be challenging enough for the average investor. Add market volatility and near-zero interest rates into the mix, and this task gets significantly harder. Savvy investors usually seek shelter by way of inflation-hedged investments. Essentially, these investments can provide a position that is either less affected or positively affected by inflation than your usual market pick.

Our Mission

Our mission at Legacy Strategic Agriculture Investments is to revolutionize the way individuals and institutions engage with agriculture as an investment avenue. We are dedicated to fostering sustainable agricultural practices, promoting global food security, and providing our clients with opportunities to reap the rewards of investing in this vital sector.

Strategic Agribusiness Investments:

Collaborate with our team of agribusiness experts to tailor investment strategies that align with your financial goals. From precision farming to value chain optimization, Legacy Strategic Agriculture Investments guides clients towards lucrative opportunities in the agriculture sector.

Comprehensive Crop Analysis:

Leverage our advanced analytical tools for in-depth crop analysis. Legacy Strategic provides clients with insights into crop selection, market trends, and yield projections, ensuring informed decision-making for optimal returns.

Sustainable Farming Initiatives:

Invest with a conscience. Legacy Strategic Agriculture Investments places a strong emphasis on sustainable and ethical farming practices. Our portfolio includes opportunities that contribute to environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and the long-term viability of agricultural investments.

Global Agriculture Market Intelligence:

Stay abreast of international agriculture markets with our global intelligence services. Legacy Strategic provides clients with real-time market data, trade insights, and geopolitical analysis, empowering them to navigate the complexities of the global agriculture landscape.
